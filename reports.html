    Report & MonitoringÌfisùn àti Àbójútó

    Report a problem and reach the council by WhatsApp, phone call, SMS, email or in person.Ròyìn ìṣòro kí o sì kàn sí ìjọba ìbílẹ̀ nípasẹ̀ WhatsApp, ìpè fóònù, SMS, ímeèlì tàbí ní ọ́fíìsì.

    Report an issueRòyìn ìṣòro

    1. WhatsAppFill the form below and send. It opens WhatsApp with your report ready.Kún fọ́ọ̀mù tó wà nísàlẹ̀, kí o sì fi ránṣẹ́. Yóò ṣí WhatsApp pẹ̀lú ìròyìn rẹ.
    2. CallPe fóònù +234 906 761 0317No WhatsApp or data? Call and give your ward, the landmark and the problem.Kò sí WhatsApp tàbí dátà? Pe fóònù, kí o sọ wọ́ọ̀dù, àmì ibi àti ìṣòro náà.
    3. SMSUse the SMS button under the form. It works on any phone.Lo bọ́tìnnì SMS tó wà lábẹ́ fọ́ọ̀mù. Ó ṣiṣẹ́ lórí fóònù yòówù.
    Call insteadPè dípò rẹ̀
    For an emergency, callFún pàjáwìrì, pe 112 first.kọ́kọ́.

    What happens nextOhun tó kàn

    1. ReceivedA ti gbà áYour report is recorded.A ti kọ ìròyìn rẹ sílẹ̀.
    2. ReviewedA ń yẹ̀ ẹ́ wòThe right department assesses it.Ẹ̀ka tó yẹ ń yẹ̀ ẹ́ wò.
    3. ActionÌṣeThe work is scheduled and done.A ṣètò iṣẹ́ náà, a sì ṣe é.
    4. ClosedA ti parí rẹ̀The council confirms the issue is resolved.Ìjọba ìbílẹ̀ jẹ́rìí pé a ti yanjú ìṣòro náà.
