    EthicsÌwà Ọmọlúàbí

    Our code of conduct, public information and a safe way to report wrongdoing.Ìlànà ìwà wa, ìròyìn gbogbo ènìyàn àti ọ̀nà ààbò láti ròyìn ìwà àìtọ́.

    Our charterÀdéhùn wa

    HonestyÒtítọ́

    No bribes, no favours and no payment for public services.

    OpennessÌfọ̀rọ̀hàn

    Budgets, receipts and contracts are open to residents.

    FairnessÒdodo

    The same service for everyone, regardless of religion, ethnicity or party.

    AccountabilityÌdáhùn fún iṣẹ́

    Every project has a named officer responsible for it.

    Public informationÌròyìn gbogbo ènìyàn

    Since the Supreme Court judgment of 11 July 2024, local government allocations are meant to be paid directly to councils. Residents have the right to ask how public money is used.

    Report a concernRòyìn ìdàníyàn

    WhatsApp and email show your number or address to the council. To stay anonymous, deliver a written report to the Secretariat.

    Request informationBéèrè fún ìròyìn

    Under the Freedom of Information Act 2011, public bodies must respond to requests within 7 days.

    Other channelsÀwọn ọ̀nà mìíràn

    You may also report corruption to the Independent Corrupt Practices Commission (ICPC) or the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC).
